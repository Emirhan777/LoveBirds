OpenStreetMap seating data

Seating data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. You may copy, redistribute, and adapt the database under that license, subject to its conditions, including attribution and share-alike requirements.

The complete machine-readable catalog below is the same dataset included in the app. It preserves original OSM object identifiers, coordinates, selected factual tags, import queries and providers, retrieval times, and upstream database timestamps. Retrieved on 18 October 2026. The total is 6,748 mapped seats, including 532 picnic tables. 671 records have a mapped numeric capacity of at least two. These bounded extracts do not claim complete city coverage. No İzmir collection is included.

The import excludes explicitly restricted, conditional-access, indoor, disused, and abandoned seating when those restrictions are mapped. Unknown access stays unverified, and restrictions on surrounding land may not appear on individual seats. Way and relation center coordinates are approximate. The data contains no live crowd, quietness, privacy, or availability measurements.

Personal finds and private notes are not part of this OpenStreetMap-derived catalog. This data license does not grant rights to unrelated application code, branding, or photographs.

Map services and corrections

Apple Maps provides native map display, place search, and walking directions. Live seating requests use the public Private.coffee Overpass service. Catalog provenance identifies the public provider used for each historical import, which may differ from the live provider.

To inspect a mapped seat, open its original source link in the app. To report a missing or incorrect object, use OpenStreetMap’s map correction options. Corrections may take time to reach providers and will not immediately change an already bundled snapshot.
