Provenance and import method

Each city record contains the exact Overpass endpoint and query, its retrieval
time, the upstream OSM database timestamp, the original copyright notice, and
counts of accepted and excluded objects. OSM type/id values are preserved, so
every record can be inspected at https://www.openstreetmap.org/{type}/{id}.

The importer (ios/scripts/fetch-catalog.py) queries bounded areas one at a time,
uses an identifying User-Agent and verified HTTPS, and pauses between requests.
The providers' documented usage policies were checked on 2026-10-18:
https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API#Public_Overpass_API_instances
https://overpass.private.coffee/

This is a seating-only extract, not a comprehensive inventory of each city.
The transformation retains original IDs, coordinates or way/relation centers,
and a short list of factual seating/access tags. It removes explicitly
restricted access, conditional access, mapped indoor seats and seats marked
disused or abandoned. It does not retain contributor identifiers, contact
details, inscriptions, social-media content or photos. The catalog contains
no invented pins or inferred seat capacities.

Missing access tags are unverified, not permission to enter private land.
An untagged restriction on a surrounding park/property may still apply.
Opening times, fees and on-site signs should be checked when visiting.
Way/relation center coordinates are approximate. Missing capacity remains
unknown. No live crowd, quietness or privacy measurement is contained in this
catalog; the presence of a mapped seat does not establish those qualities.
