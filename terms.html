Terms of use

LoveBirds helps you discover mapped seating and keep your own observations. Please use it with care for the places and people around you.

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · LoveBirds for iOS, version 1.0

About the service

LoveBirds is an iPhone app provided by Emirhan Şimşek. It displays OpenStreetMap-derived seating, uses Apple Maps for map features, and stores your personal collection on your device. These terms describe use of the app and website. Where you obtain the app through Apple, Apple’s applicable store terms and standard licensed application agreement also apply unless a separate applicable agreement is provided.

Free and Premium

Free includes all bundled city seat collections, map and place search, capacity and basic seating filters, up to 10 saved places and 3 personal finds, visit notes, and fresh seat searches up to a 3 km radius. Premium provides unlimited saved places and personal finds, advanced quiet-potential, secluded-setting, water, and backrest filters, and fresh seat searches up to 10 km. Both plans use the same underlying mapped information and its limitations.

You can use Free without subscribing. No LoveBirds account is required. The support page explains the plans and collection limits.

Subscriptions and billing

Premium is offered through Apple as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Both billing periods provide the same Premium features. The purchase screen displays the actual localized price returned by the App Store and the billing period before you confirm. Annual plans are charged as the full annual amount shown, once per year; they are not monthly installment payments. Any displayed annual saving compares that annual charge with 12 monthly payments in the same currency.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal. The App Store’s confirmation and subscription-management screens show the applicable charge and renewal information. Product availability, taxes, and prices may vary by storefront. Applicable price-change notices and consent requirements are handled through Apple.

To manage or cancel, choose Premium → Manage existing subscription in LoveBirds, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple shows when a cancellation takes effect. Deleting or offloading the app does not cancel your subscription. See Apple’s cancellation instructions. Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled through Apple’s purchase support, subject to Apple’s policies and your applicable consumer rights.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase to restore an active subscription. Restoring paid access does not transfer locally stored places or notes. Subscription expiry does not delete your existing saved places, personal finds, or visit notes. You can still view and remove existing entries; Free limits apply to new additions, advanced filters turn off, and the fresh-search radius is limited to 3 km.

The Apple Standard EULA applies to the licensed app. Nothing in these terms removes rights provided by applicable consumer law.

Mapped information has limits

Seat locations, capacities, access details, and opening hours depend on the available source data and may be incomplete, approximate, outdated, or incorrect. A capacity of two or more does not establish that an unobstructed seat is available for you both. Quiet potential is an estimate from mapped surroundings, not a measurement of present noise, crowds, privacy, or safety. A saved observation reflects your own visit.

The included city collections are dated, bounded extracts. They are not complete city inventories or recent inspections. Images are illustrative and do not depict the exact mapped seating. LoveBirds does not reserve a seat or guarantee access, solitude, availability, or any particular experience.

Respect the place

Check current signs, opening times, fees, access restrictions, weather, and conditions before visiting. Do not enter private or restricted property without permission. Known restrictions are filtered where mapped, but an absent restriction is not permission. Respect other visitors and leave the location as you found it.

Your personal information and sharing

You control the finds and notes you enter. Do not use the app to expose someone else’s private location or personal information. Review a place before sharing its map link with another service or person. Read the privacy policy for local storage, location use, external providers, and deletion choices.

External services and availability

Apple Maps, public map-data providers, hosting services, and external websites have their own terms and privacy policies. Network features depend on service availability and coverage. Providers may limit requests, change, or stop operating. The app may retain existing results when a live search fails. Use the bundled city collections when live seating requests are unavailable.
