LoveBirds support

Find answers, report a map issue, or get in touch about the iPhone app.

Contact the developer

LoveBirds is developed by Emirhan Şimşek. For a bug, feature request, accessibility issue, or general question, email pendrawapp@gmail.com.

Email LoveBirds support ↗

Include your iOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue. Send only the information needed to help with your request, and crop identifying details out of screenshots.

You can also open a public GitHub issue. GitHub issues require a GitHub account and are visible to everyone. Do not post private locations, personal notes, contact details, or other sensitive information there.

Common questions

How do I find a place where two people can sit?

Choose Cities to open İstanbul, Ankara, Antalya, or London, then use the seating filters. The capacity filter includes seats whose mapped numeric capacity is at least two, or a personal find you confirmed. Unknown capacities are excluded. Check the individual place when you arrive: a mapped count does not guarantee an unobstructed shared seat.

Are these places guaranteed to be quiet or isolated?

No. The included city collections contain mapped seating without live crowd, noise, or privacy measurements. For supported live searches, quiet potential is estimated from mapped roads and busy amenities. It is not a live observation. Your own visit notes can help you remember how a place felt on a particular day.

Why are there no results with Extra quiet or Secluded selected?

The bundled city data does not establish those qualities, so unknown places may be excluded by these filters. Clear the filters to see mapped seats, or try Search this area for current mapped context. Coverage varies, and missing information remains unknown.

Why does a map pin have a generic bench photo?

Photos are illustrative images, not photographs of the exact mapped seat. Each real OpenStreetMap result links to its original source record. The catalog import date is a data retrieval date, not a physical inspection.

How can I use the app without sharing my location?

Open Cities and select a collection, type a city or place, or move the map manually. You can deny the location prompt and still use these features. Live searches send an approximate search area to the seating provider; Apple map display and place searches use Apple services. See the privacy policy for details.

Why does live seating search fail or take a while?

Live search uses a public map-data service that can be busy or unavailable. Try again later or reduce the radius. Existing results remain visible after a failed request. The four included city seat lists remain available without a live seating request; map tiles require an internet connection or previously cached tiles.

How do I switch cities or find seats elsewhere?

Tap Cities to switch between the four included collections. Exact searches for their city names also open those collections. For another area, search a place or move the map and choose Search this area. Availability depends on OpenStreetMap coverage and the live provider.

Where are my saved places and notes stored?

They are stored in the app’s local storage on your iPhone. There is no LoveBirds account or shared review feed. Device backups may include that data. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading it preserves that data. See your privacy choices for individual removal and backup details.

A seat is missing, inaccessible, or on private land. What should I do?

Follow on-site signs, opening hours, and local access rules. A missing access tag does not grant permission to enter. Open the seat’s original OpenStreetMap record to inspect it, and use OpenStreetMap’s correction options to report a public map error. You can also send us the public OSM record link through support. Included snapshots only change with a later app/data update.

Can I share a personal find?

The Share action sends the selected spot’s name and map link through the iOS share sheet. Choose the recipient carefully. Your private visit notes are not included in that spot link. Mapped-data exports exclude personal finds and notes.

Which iPhones are supported?

LoveBirds requires iOS 17 or later. It uses native iPhone controls, supports light and dark appearance, and includes Dynamic Type and VoiceOver labels. Report an accessibility problem through support with the screen and setting involved.

About the data

The included collections contain 6,748 OpenStreetMap seats retrieved on 18 October 2026. These are bounded extracts, not complete inventories of each city. See Data & credits for source information, attribution, licensing, and the downloadable catalog.
