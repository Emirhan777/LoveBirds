Privacy policy

Your saved places and personal notes are stored on your device. Online maps and live seating searches use external services, as explained below.

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · LoveBirds for iOS, version 1.0

Who provides LoveBirds

LoveBirds is provided by Emirhan Şimşek. This policy covers the LoveBirds iPhone app and its public support website. There is no LoveBirds account system, advertising, analytics SDK, advertising identifier, or developer-operated service that receives your saved places or visit notes. Optional subscriptions use your Apple Account through Apple’s App Store. We do not sell your personal data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores saved seating records, your personal finds (including names and coordinates), visit observations and notes, and your selected city in local iOS app storage. These support your personal collection and restore it between launches. They are not uploaded to a LoveBirds account or shared review database.

iOS or a backup service you enable may include this app data in device backups. A restored backup may restore previously saved information. Apple controls those backup processes. Live search results may also be cached temporarily on your device to avoid repeating requests.

Optional Premium purchases

Apple handles subscription payments, billing, and purchase history using your Apple Account. LoveBirds uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to load subscription products, request a purchase you choose, restore purchases, and open Apple’s subscription management screen. Apple’s processing is described in App Store & Privacy.

The app processes Apple-verified transaction and subscription information on your device to determine whether Premium is active. This includes product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, and relevant renewal or revocation status. StoreKit may provide signed, cached transaction information while offline. LoveBirds has no developer-operated purchase backend and does not receive your payment-card number, Apple Account password, or payment credentials.

Your saved places, personal finds, and visit notes are not included in purchase or restore requests. Restoring Premium does not synchronize that collection between devices. When a subscription expires, the app changes which features are available; it does not delete your local collection. You manage the subscription through Apple separately from deleting local app data.

Location and online services

Location access is optional. LoveBirds asks for permission when you choose the location button and requests a location while the app is in use. The app does not track your location in the background. You can browse a city collection or choose an area without granting location permission.

Service or featureInformation involved and its purpose
Live seating searchThe search center is rounded to a grid of 0.01 degrees (roughly 1 km, varying by latitude) before it is sent to the public Private.coffee Overpass service. The request includes that approximate area, search radius, and map-data query. The provider also receives your IP address, request timing, and an app-identifying User-Agent as part of the connection. This allows it to return nearby mapped seating; it does not receive your saved-place list or private notes.
Apple MapsMap display, place search, and walking directions use Apple services. Information such as search text, displayed map area, requested destination, and location or network information needed for those features may be processed by Apple. Its handling of that information is governed by Apple Maps & Privacy.
Sharing a spotWhen you choose Share, the app passes the selected place’s name and map link (including coordinates) to the system share sheet. The app or person you choose receives that information. Review the recipient before sharing a personal find.
Exporting mapped dataThe catalog and mapped-result export contain OpenStreetMap-derived data. Personal finds, saved-place selections, and private visit notes are excluded from those exports.

The rounded live-search area can still reveal the neighborhood you are exploring. The exact location available to LoveBirds remains on the device for local calculations; Apple’s map services and sharing actions are separate, as described above. Seat coordinates returned by the provider describe mapped objects, rather than a record of your movements.

Private.coffee’s published policy describes retaining request metadata, including a truncated IP address, for up to 48 hours to diagnose and mitigate technical issues. Read the Private.coffee privacy policy for the provider’s current practices and contact details. LoveBirds does not control provider logs or their deletion.

The bundled city catalog was retrieved in advance from public OpenStreetMap services. Browsing the included seat records does not send a new seating query. Historical provider names in that catalog describe its import provenance, not an additional service contacted during offline browsing.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

  • Turn off location access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → LoveBirds. City browsing and manual area selection remain available.
  • Unsave a place using its heart button. Delete a personal find from Your finds. If you also saved that find, remove the saved copy separately.
  • To clear a visit’s text, edit the note and save it with an empty text field; observations can be reset to Unknown. Local records otherwise remain until you change or remove them.
  • To remove all local LoveBirds data, delete the app using iOS’s Delete App action. Offloading an app preserves its documents and data. Manage existing device backups separately in your backup settings.
  • You choose whether to open external links, share a place, export mapped data, or send a support request.

We cannot retrieve your local notes or location history from a developer account because LoveBirds does not upload them to one. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond and troubleshoot. Please send only what is needed.

This website and external links

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, forms, or remote font services. Images and styles are served with the site. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to provide hosting, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support issues submitted on GitHub are public and are processed by GitHub. Do not include private locations, personal notes, contact details, passwords, or other sensitive information in an issue. External websites, including OpenStreetMap, Apple, Instagram, and photo-credit links, follow their own policies once you open them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app’s data handling changes and revise the date above. A change that requires permission will be handled through the appropriate app or system prompt.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to LoveBirds support, email Emirhan Şimşek at pendrawapp@gmail.com. Send only the details needed to understand your request. The support page also offers a public GitHub issue option; do not include personal information in a public issue.

Requests concerning a provider’s own records should also be directed to that provider using the policy links above. Instructions for removing information stored in the app are in Your choices, retention, and deletion.
